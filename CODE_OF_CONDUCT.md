# Code of Conduct

PeerBeam welcomes people of every experience level and background. Treat participants with respect, give constructive technical feedback, credit others' work, and make room for different perspectives. Disagreement about code is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe misconduct. Decisions should consider impact and context and explain the action to the affected participant when safe. Maintainers are also subject to this code.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a contact method listed on their public profile. Do not publish sensitive allegations or personal information in an issue. If no private contact is listed, request a private channel without identifying affected people. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves from the decision.
